How FoodChain works

One connected journey. Four simple stages.

Physical supply-chain events are connected to digital records, making the journey easier to monitor, audit and verify.

01

Capture

ESP32 devices collect environmental and location data from the physical supply chain.

IoT telemetry
02

Transmit

Sensor readings travel through MQTT into the FoodChain backend for processing and storage.

MQTT
03

Secure

Supply-chain transactions are recorded through Hyperledger Fabric for tamper-evident verification.

Blockchain
04

Verify

A QR code gives consumers a simple public view of the verified batch journey.
