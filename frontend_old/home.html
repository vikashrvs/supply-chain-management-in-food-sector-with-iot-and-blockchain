Enterprise Stack

POWERED BY TECHNOLOGY

Enterprise-grade stack engineered for food safety and traceability

📡

IOT SENSORS

DHT22 & GPS sensors monitor temperature, humidity and location at every supply chain stage in real-time.

📨

MQTT PROTOCOL

Mosquitto broker ensures real-time, lightweight data transmission from sensors to backend with sub-second latency.

⚡

EDGE COMPUTING

Raspberry Pi processes data locally — reducing latency and ensuring offline resilience with edge intelligence.

🔗

HYPERLEDGER FABRIC

Private blockchain ensures tamper-proof, immutable records accessible only to authorized supply chain parties.

📜

SMART CONTRACTS

Automated validation rules ensure data integrity before any record is committed to the distributed ledger.

📱

QR TRACEABILITY

Consumers scan QR codes to view complete product journey from farm to their hands in seconds.
